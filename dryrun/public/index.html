From idea to a running product

  1. Bring your own data

    Upload price books, claims and member files. Names, phone numbers and ID numbers are removed on upload.

  2. Rehearse several versions

    Adjudication, faster payment, member plan choice and campaigns. Compare runs side by side. The Coach suggests the version that does best.

  3. Ask the people you serve

    Send a short survey by SMS or WhatsApp and feed the answers straight into the next run.

  4. Know what needs the regulator

    Every change is sorted: no approval needed, notify only, or regulator approval needed. Your compliance team keeps the final say.

  5. Go live with us beside you

    Dry Run Live runs the programme against guardrails, compares actuals to the forecast and flags drift. We can operate it for the first year.

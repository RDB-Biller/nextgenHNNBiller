Two ways to settle a bill

However a patient is covered, the money goes directly to the clinic's account. The platform routes and records it — it is never in the flow of funds.

01 Patient self-pay

Mobile Money, debit or credit card, or cash — captured against the itemised bill and reconciled instantly.

02 Payer A2A unique

The patient names their payer; the clinic taps it. The payer authorises a transfer from its own Stanbic account straight to the clinic.

Tap the insurer. The money moves itself.

The bill, with the patient's member ID, is routed to the named payer for validation. A secure link (or their own system) lets them authorise an account-to-account transfer over the Stanbic SBG rail — and the clinic, patient, payer, and any sponsoring employer are all notified the moment it clears.

Tracked claims

Cashback is the refund received from the sponsoring insurer (up to 100% of the NHIS amount). If the refund lands with the provider, a member-charged margin is netted from the cashback and the balance is owed on to the member. If the insurer pays the member directly, nothing is owed on and the margin is invoiced instead. Set the margin in the Revenue Console.
